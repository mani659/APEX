# RC007 Study 008: Recovery Contribution De-Conflation

## Scientific Context
Following the determination in RC007 Study 007 that the Apex entry signal has negative standalone expectancy, this study was executed to de-conflate the components of the Apex Version 1 recovery architecture to identify exactly where the historical profitability originated.

## Methodology
The 410 qualified, isolated entries from Study 007 (canonical EURUSD dataset) were replayed under two models:
- **Model A:** Initial Entry Only (Fixed 240-bar Observation window, simulating 'No Recovery').
- **Model B:** Production Recovery Architecture (Normal Mode, utilizing full Inventory Management rules including grid expansion, R-Multiple payoffs, and inventory decay/aging exits).

Both runs were perfectly synchronized via historical timestamps to calculate the isolated vs. recovered PnL per event.

## Critical Findings

### 1. The Grid Illusion
The data conclusively proved that the Grid Expansion logic **never actually triggered**. The average number of additional entries was 0.00, and the maximum inventory depth across the entire EURUSD baseline remained strictly 1. 

Because grid expansion relies on a new behavioural event being triggered to add to the basket, and such events require specific `3 * ATR` displacements from the open price, secondary entries rarely if ever materialize under the frozen rules.

### 2. The True Source of Historical Profitability
Since grid expansion contributed precisely 0.00000 additional PnL, the entirety of the historical positive expectancy was generated by the **R-Multiple Payoff Architecture and Aging Exits** (Inventory Management layer). 

By acting as an asymmetric exit filter, `InventoryManagement` harvested small, immediate favorable excursions (MFE) aggressively, securing an 89.0% win rate. It allowed adverse excursions (MAE) to run deep, exiting them via aging time limits or deep stops. 

### 3. Tail-Risk Profile
The strategy relies entirely on rescuing negative-expectancy entries via asymmetric profit-taking. As a result, the risk profile is extremely negatively skewed. The worst single-trade outcome observed was an un-expanded loss of -0.06983 (almost 700 pips), demonstrating that while the win rate is artificially high, the tail-risk explosion when mean-reversion fails is catastrophic.

## Success Definition Answered

> "The historical Apex profitability is primarily generated by **the asymmetric exit architecture (R-Multiple profit taking and aging limits), which artificially inflates win rate at the cost of severe, negatively-skewed tail risk, while the grid expansion module contributes absolutely nothing.**"
