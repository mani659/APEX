# RC013 Final Closure & Repository Commit
## Objective
Formally close and freeze the RC013 Session / Calendar Structural Mechanics campaign after completion of Studies 001–004. 
The purpose of this operation is to preserve the validated scientific knowledge, reject the failed monetization architecture, cleanly mark the campaign as closed, and prepare Apex for the next research direction.

## Final Scientific Status
**RC013 STATUS: CLOSED — STRUCTURAL PRIMITIVE VALIDATED, MONETIZATION ARCHITECTURE REJECTED**

### VALIDATED
The deterministic session/transition structural effect.
**Evidence:**
* RC013 Study 001 discovered persistent session-dependent distributional differences.
* RC013 Study 002 independently validated the candidates using a clean chronological discovery/validation split.
* RC013 Study 003 demonstrated the associated path geometry.
* The structural effect is direction-neutral and does not depend on localized directional prediction.

### REJECTED
The specific raw immediate range-breakout monetization architecture tested in RC013 Study 004.
**Evidence:**
* `ASIA_TO_LONDON` expectancy: approximately -0.99 pips.
* `LONDON_NY_OVERLAP` expectancy: approximately -1.54 pips.
* Both candidates had negative Profit Factor.
* Tail losses remained material.
* Lowering friction to 0.5 pip did not make `ASIA_TO_LONDON` positive.

### NOT PROVEN
* That all session-based trading is impossible.
* That no future session monetization architecture can work.
* That session effects are causal because of institutional liquidity provision.
* That the session primitive itself is a profitable strategy.

**The final scientific conclusion is:**
> **The session-transition structural information is validated, but the tested raw range-breakout architecture does not monetize it profitably.**

## Permanent Knowledge to Preserve
1. Deterministic session transitions materially alter EURUSD future movement distributions.
2. `LONDON_NY_OVERLAP` is a validated structural primitive.
3. `ASIA_TO_LONDON` is a validated structural primitive.
4. The session effects survived independent chronological validation.
5. The effects are approximately direction-neutral.
6. Session expansion exhibits higher movement with directionally efficient path geometry relative to the HIGH_VOL chop regime.
7. The raw immediate pre-session-range breakout architecture does not generate positive expectancy.
8. The breakout architecture is affected by negative expectancy and significant tail-loss exposure.
9. Transaction-cost reduction alone does not rescue the tested architecture.
10. The validated session primitive must therefore be treated as **information**, not as a validated trading strategy.

## Methodological Boundaries & Future Research
The next research direction must come from a separate project-level decision. RC013 is formally frozen to prevent parameter optimization and research drift.

## Final Decision
**RC013 established that deterministic session transitions create a robust, direction-neutral structural change in the future EURUSD movement distribution. Independent validation confirmed the effect. Path analysis showed directionally efficient expansion relative to the previously studied HIGH_VOL chop regime. However, the tested raw session-range breakout architecture produced negative expectancy and significant tail losses. Therefore the session effect is preserved as validated structural information, while the tested breakout monetization architecture is rejected. The campaign is closed to prevent parameter optimization and research drift.**
