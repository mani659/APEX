# Findings: Continuation Expectancy

## Overview

By analyzing the existing regime metrics, we evaluated the expectancy and consistency of continuation across different market conditions. The complete data tables can be found in `Tables.md`.

## Where continuation works

Continuation works best in high-momentum environments, particularly during London and New York overlaps where directional volatility expands.

## Where continuation fails

Continuation fails during low-volatility Asian sessions and transitioning ranges, where mean-reversion dominates.

## Regimes for future investigation

High-volatility persistence regimes show strong out-of-sample promise and require deeper execution analysis.

## Regimes to avoid

Low-volatility chop and immediate baseline transitions yield negative expectancy and should be strictly avoided.
