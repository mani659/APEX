# RC013 Study 000 — Data Qualification for Structural Carry & Funding

## 1. Current Apex Evidence Base
The Apex research programme (RC007–RC012) definitively demonstrated that short-term directional alpha on M1 is effectively zero under the tested formulations, and that a massive non-directional volatility edge (HIGH_VOL) exists but is destroyed by spot retail execution mechanics (truncation and whipsaw). The strategic pivot requires finding a persistent structural source of return that exists *before* predicting direction, such as financing premiums, to provide a baseline positive expectancy.

## 2. Structural Carry/Funding Hypothesis
> **Can persistent financing, carry, or funding differences create a measurable positive baseline expectancy that can be harvested without requiring short-term directional prediction?**

## 3. Candidate Domains
Based on the RC013 Charter, three candidate domains are conceptually available:
1. **FX Carry / Swap:** Capturing the interest rate differential between two currencies (e.g., long a high-yield currency against a low-yield currency).
2. **Crypto Funding:** Capturing the perpetual futures funding rate (e.g., shorting a coin on perps where funding is structurally positive while holding the spot asset to remain delta-neutral).
3. **Cross-Asset Financing Differences:** Arbitraging financing costs across different instruments mapping to the same underlying asset.

## 4. Existing Data Availability
An exhaustive audit of the existing Apex repository (`data/`, `datasets/`, `config/`, and `mt5/` directories) yields the following results regarding historical carry/funding data:

- **Historical swap rates:** **UNAVAILABLE**
- **Historical funding rates:** **UNAVAILABLE**
- **Financing costs:** **UNAVAILABLE**
- **Contract specifications:** **UNAVAILABLE**
- **Trading-session rules:** **UNAVAILABLE**
- **Rollover timing:** **UNAVAILABLE**
- **Leverage/margin assumptions:** **UNAVAILABLE**
- **Historical prices needed to construct the hedge:** **AVAILABLE** (High-quality M1 OHLCV for EURUSD, XAUUSD, XAGUSD, BTCUSD, USATECHIDXUSD).

Currently, the Apex repository only possesses directional price history (OHLCV). There are no historical metadata files, no swap schedules, and no historical funding rate logs. 

## 5. Data Limitations
The absolute absence of historical swap rates and funding rates completely halts the ability to conduct rigorous carry research. It is mathematically impossible to measure the persistence, duration, or regime changes of a financing premium if the historical time series of that premium does not exist in the database. Furthermore, applying *current* broker swap rates to *historical* price data introduces a catastrophic look-ahead bias and ignores central bank interest rate cycles.

## 6. Economic Definitions
To maintain scientific rigor in future carry evaluations:
- **Carry Yield:** The gross financing/swap received or paid for holding the position.
- **Price Return:** The PnL generated by the movement in the underlying spot price.
- **Total Return:** `Carry Yield + Price Return - Transaction Costs`.

*A positive Carry Yield is meaningless if the Price Return reliably decays by an equal or greater amount (Uncovered Interest Rate Parity), or if Transaction Costs consume the yield.*

## 7. Market-Neutral Requirements
If pursuing a market-neutral funding capture strategy (e.g., Crypto Cash-and-Carry), the evaluation must explicitly track:
- **Long exposure:** (e.g., BTCUSD Spot)
- **Offsetting short exposure:** (e.g., BTCUSD Perpetual Futures)
- **Hedge ratio:** Exactly 1:1 nominal value.
- **Financing received:** Daily/Hourly funding payments from the short perp.
- **Financing paid:** Spot holding costs/margin interest.
- **Transaction costs:** Spread crossing on both legs, execution commissions.
- **Residual directional exposure:** Slippage risk on liquidation or un-pegging risk.

## 8. Total-Cost Framework
The net economic expectancy of any carry strategy must incorporate:
1. Gross financing/carry received.
2. Minus spread (often widened during rollover).
3. Minus commissions.
4. Minus execution slippage.
5. Minus hedge execution costs (if market neutral).

## 9. Candidate Qualification Criteria
A domain can only be nominated for RC013 Study 001 if it possesses:
1. Trustworthy historical financing data.
2. Clear economic mechanics.
3. Realistic execution viability.
4. Measurable net-return potential.

## 10. Final Recommendation for RC013 Study 001

**DECISION: PAUSE RC013 AND RETURN TO STRATEGIC REVIEW**

Currently, **NO DOMAIN** meets the candidate qualification criteria because the foundational prerequisite—trustworthy historical data of swap rates or funding rates—is missing from the Apex repository. 

We cannot force the research to continue without data. The next immediate project-level action must be to either:
1. Formally authorize the acquisition and engineering required to ingest historical MT5 swap rates and/or crypto perpetual funding rates, OR
2. Acknowledge the data limitation and pivot the strategic review to a different structural edge that utilizes the existing OHLCV pricing data (e.g., Statistical Relative Value / Cointegration).

RC013 is paused pending the resolution of the data availability constraint.
