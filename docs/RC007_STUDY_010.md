# RC007 Study 010: Campaign Closure & Hypothesis Reset

## 1. Original RC007 Question
> Does the Apex behavioural entry signal possess statistically significant alpha independent of grid-based recovery mechanics?

## 2. Final Evidence Chain

### Study 007
The corrected Entry Isolation replay demonstrated:
- Valid behavioural events: 5,235
- Executable candidates: 410
- Standalone win rate: 35.1%
- Negative mean PnL: -0.00048
- Unfavorable MAE/MFE relationship: Mean MAE (0.00132) exceeded Mean MFE (0.00059) by more than 2x.

### Study 008
The recovery de-conflation demonstrated:
- Additional grid entries were effectively zero (Average: 0.00).
- Maximum inventory depth was exactly 1.
- Grid expansion contributed approximately nothing to the observed results.
- The historical result was not being produced by grid recovery.

### Study 009
The exit de-conflation demonstrated:
- Symmetric reference exits remain negative (Win rate 28.5%, Expectancy -0.00012).
- The frozen Apex exit architecture creates a high win rate (89.0%) through strong payoff asymmetry.
- Losses are highly negatively skewed (worst 5% of losses generate 88% of total loss footprint).
- The apparent historical profitability is not evidence of durable predictive alpha.
- Tail losses dominate the risk distribution (1 max loss wipes out ~46 average winners).

## 3. Final Scientific Conclusion

### Signal Edge
Current evidence does NOT support intrinsic positive alpha in the frozen behavioural entry.

### Recovery Edge
Grid expansion contributes effectively zero under the frozen Version 1 configuration.

### Exit Architecture
The historical high win rate is produced by asymmetric payoff construction and is accompanied by severe negatively-skewed tail risk.

### Overall Apex V1 Hypothesis
The current Version 1 architecture does NOT possess a validated durable trading edge.

## 4. What Has Been Disproved
1. A 3.0 ATR event alone is not a sufficient trading signal.
2. Low Participation + Stabilization does not establish standalone positive entry alpha.
3. Grid recovery does not explain the historical profitability under the frozen configuration.
4. High win rate is not sufficient evidence of a viable edge.
5. The frozen asymmetric exit architecture can create an attractive win rate while hiding severe tail risk.
6. The historical V1 profitability cannot currently be interpreted as validated alpha.

## 5. What Has NOT Been Disproved
### Rejected
The current trading formulation.

### Still Unknown
The broader market-behaviour hypothesis. 
It has NOT been concluded that extreme displacement is meaningless, exhaustion does not exist, market context cannot improve outcomes, selective trading cannot work, or cross-market behaviour is impossible. Only the current V1 formulation was tested and rejected.

## 6. Scientific Lessons
### Lesson 1
A strong-looking win rate can be manufactured by payoff asymmetry.
### Lesson 2
Entry quality must be measured independently of recovery and exits.
### Lesson 3
A grid that never activates cannot be credited for historical profitability.
### Lesson 4
MAE/MFE distribution is more informative than win rate alone.
### Lesson 5
Negative results are valuable because they eliminate false explanations.
### Lesson 6
Apex must not mistake a successful payoff distribution for predictive alpha.

## 7. Research Reset
> The next campaign must begin from the empirical fact that the current V1 entry/exit formulation does not contain validated standalone alpha.

Future hypotheses must therefore be independently justified rather than being incremental modifications of the rejected formulation.
