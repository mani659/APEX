# RC009 Discovery Campaign — FREEZE DOCUMENT

## Campaign Objective
Determine whether isolated EURUSD M1 behavior possesses structural predictability by evaluating local sequences, participation behaviors, cross-market synchronization, and higher-timeframe regime conditioning.

## Studies Completed
- **Study 001:** Local Behavioral Candidate Discovery (Momentum, Absorption, Volatility Compression, Flag)
- **Study 002:** Methodology Audit & Blind Spot Identification
- **Study 003:** State Sequence Analysis (N=3, N=5 contiguous blocks)
- **Study 004:** Cross-Market Synchronization (XAUUSD, XAGUSD, BTCUSD, USATECHIDXUSD)
- **Study 005:** Formal Campaign Review & Blind Spot Closure Assessment
- **Study 006:** Higher-Timeframe (H4) Regime Conditioning

## Major Findings & Rejected Hypotheses
1. **Local Behavioral Patterns:** The four pre-declared local formulations did not demonstrate meaningful predictive structure.
2. **State Sequences:** N=3 and N=5 M1 state sequences did not add meaningful information beyond the terminal state itself.
3. **Cross-Market Synchronization:** Contemporaneous and short-lag (t-15, t-30) state information from context markets did not provide robust incremental information beyond EURUSD's own state.
4. **H4 Regime Conditioning:** H4 volatility/direction conditioning did not materially alter M1 state outcomes.

## Methodological Limitations
- The campaign restricted its search entirely to non-overlapping 15-bar M1 blocks and explicit 9-state (Volatility/Direction) vocabulary.
- It explicitly excluded Machine Learning, portfolio optimization, parameter scanning, and non-linear combinations.
- The rejection is strictly limited to the tested behavioral dimensions and formulations.

## Final Scientific Conclusion
No robust predictive behavioral structure was discovered within the tested RC009 behavioral dimensions on EURUSD M1. The local, sequential, cross-market, and higher-timeframe hypotheses evaluated did not produce actionable alpha.

## Final Status
**RC009 STATUS: FROZEN — NEGATIVE DISCOVERY CAMPAIGN**

No further RC009 studies are authorized.
