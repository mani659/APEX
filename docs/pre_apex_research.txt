PHASE 7 — PAYOFF ARCHITECTURE DISCOVERY
A major conceptual shift occurred.
Observation:
Later grid levels contain stronger reversion energy.
Example:
If price reaches level 7 or 8 and reverts only partially:
late entries become highly profitable,
early inventory can recover.
This led to the development of:
R-Multiple Payoff Architecture
Instead of fixed TP:
Each level receives its own payoff multiple.
Example:
Level 1 → 1R
Level 2 → 1.2R
Level 3 → 1.5R
Level 4 → 2R
...
This transformed the payoff geometry.
Major breakthrough result:
Win rate ≈ 87.5%
Massive profitability
Extremely low drawdown
High inventory recovery efficiency
This was the first major evidence that:
Payoff engineering may matter more than entry engineering.
