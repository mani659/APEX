# RC007 Study 000: Campaign Definition & Research Charter

## Campaign
**RC007 — Edge De-conflation & Statistical Proof**

## Status
**PLANNED**

---

# Scientific Objective

Before commencing RC007, formally define the scientific question, scope, assumptions, success criteria, and constitutional boundaries for the campaign.

The objective of this study is **not** to perform research, modify the engine, or optimize parameters.

Its sole purpose is to ensure that RC007 begins with a single, measurable hypothesis and remains protected from feature creep, parameter drift, and over-engineering.

---

# Background

RC001–RC006 established:

- Behavioural theory
- Frozen mathematical primitives
- Software architecture
- Deterministic Python Reference Engine
- Scientific telemetry
- Research platform validation

The platform is therefore considered scientifically qualified.

RC007 becomes the first campaign whose research subject is **the trading edge itself**, rather than the software.

---

# Primary Research Question

**Does the Apex behavioural entry signal possess statistically significant alpha independent of grid-based recovery mechanics?**

This is the **only** research question RC007 is permitted to answer.

---

# Scientific Hypothesis

## Null Hypothesis (H₀)

The observed profitability of Apex is primarily explained by inventory management and grid recovery rather than the initial behavioural entry.

## Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)

The behavioural entry itself contains measurable predictive power, even when evaluated independently of grid expansion.

---

# Campaign Scope

RC007 SHALL:

- Disable grid expansion.
- Evaluate single-entry trades only.
- Analyse Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE).
- Analyse Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE).
- Analyse holding time.
- Analyse expectancy.
- Analyse stop-loss behaviour.
- Analyse take-profit behaviour.
- Quantify raw entry quality.

RC007 SHALL NOT:

- Optimise parameters.
- Introduce Machine Learning.
- Introduce Artificial Intelligence.
- Introduce cross-asset adaptation.
- Introduce market-context filters.
- Modify frozen behavioural primitives.
- Modify telemetry architecture.
- Modify runtime architecture.

---

# Success Criteria

RC007 will be considered successful if it produces:

- Statistically valid MAE profile.
- Statistically valid MFE profile.
- Holding-time distribution.
- Expectancy distribution.
- Raw entry efficiency metrics.
- Evidence supporting or rejecting intrinsic entry alpha.

---

# Expected Deliverables

- Entry Isolation Report
- MAE Statistical Profile
- MFE Statistical Profile
- Holding Time Distribution
- Expectancy Analysis
- Raw Entry Alpha Assessment

---

# Constitutional Constraints

Throughout RC007:

- RC001–RC006 remain frozen.
- The Python Reference Engine remains unchanged except for isolated experimental configuration.
- No optimisation is permitted.
- No parameter tuning is permitted.
- Negative findings are considered scientifically valid outcomes.
- Research conclusions must be supported by evidence only.

---

# Exit Criteria

RC007 concludes when one of the following statements can be supported by statistical evidence:

## Outcome A

**The behavioural entry possesses intrinsic statistical alpha independent of grid recovery.**

OR

## Outcome B

**The behavioural entry does not possess sufficient standalone alpha, indicating that future research should focus on behavioural context rather than entry mechanics.**

---

# Expected Transition

If RC007 confirms intrinsic entry alpha:

→ Proceed to **RC008 — Behavioural Universalization**

If RC007 rejects intrinsic entry alpha:

→ Reframe the research programme around **context-dependent behavioural selection** before attempting cross-asset expansion.

---

# Campaign Philosophy

RC007 intentionally answers **one question only**.

It does **not** attempt to improve profitability, optimise parameters, or redesign the strategy.

The purpose is to reduce uncertainty by isolating and measuring the statistical contribution of the behavioural entry itself.

Only after the raw entry has been scientifically validated (or rejected) may future campaigns investigate universalization, market context, execution degradation, or live deployment.

---

# Final Recommendation

This charter formally constrains RC007 to a single scientific objective.

By freezing the campaign definition before analysis begins, Apex preserves the discipline established throughout RC001–RC006 and minimizes the risk of scope drift, feature creep, parameter optimisation, and over-engineering.

---

# Final Verdict

**APPROVED**

RC007 is authorized to begin under this charter.

Any work falling outside this scope shall be deferred to future research campaigns.
