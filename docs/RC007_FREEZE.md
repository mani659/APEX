# Final Scientific Findings

## Entry

The isolated behavioural entry demonstrated negative expectancy.

The entry therefore does not currently possess validated standalone alpha.

---

## Grid / Recovery

Under the frozen Version 1 architecture:

- Additional grid entries were effectively zero.
- Grid expansion did not materially contribute to historical profitability.

The grid must therefore NOT be credited as the source of the observed historical performance.

---

## Exit Architecture

The frozen asymmetric exit architecture produced:

- high apparent win rate;
- many small favorable outcomes;
- relatively few but very large adverse outcomes;
- severe negative skew;
- substantial tail-risk concentration.

The historical high win rate therefore cannot be treated as evidence of durable alpha.

---

# Final RC007 Conclusion

The current Apex Version 1 behavioural entry + grid + exit formulation does **not** demonstrate validated durable trading alpha.

The previous apparent profitability was primarily associated with asymmetric payoff packaging rather than a demonstrated predictive entry edge.

# Knowledge Permanently Preserved

RC007 establishes the following permanent conclusions:

1. A 3.0 ATR behavioural event is not sufficient as a standalone trading signal.
2. Participation + stabilization, under the current frozen formulation, does not establish standalone positive entry alpha.
3. Grid expansion contributes effectively nothing under the frozen Version 1 configuration.
4. High win rate alone is not evidence of a viable trading edge.
5. Asymmetric exits can create an attractive win rate while concealing severe tail risk.
6. Entry quality, recovery contribution, and exit contribution must always be de-conflated when evaluating strategy performance.
7. The current Version 1 formulation must not be incrementally "rescued" by arbitrary filters or parameter tuning.
